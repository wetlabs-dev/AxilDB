# Care Cadence Research

Care research generates persistent proposals. It never activates a schedule or changes specimen overrides, disabled activities, care events or initial due dates. Approval writes the same definition cadence used by manual configuration and dynamic inheritance.

## Research and review

Server administrators with verified two-factor authentication can open **Server Management → Care Cadence Research**. Research shows actual missing-activity counts, dependency blocks, previous applicability decisions and persistent job progress. Review groups proposals by definition or shows a compact table, with definition/activity/confidence/status/source filters and name/activity/date/confidence sorting. History retains decisions and original recommendations.

**Research Missing Cadences** also revisits insufficient-information findings: research completion does not mean a care schedule was configured. Unresolved pending findings remain visible in Review with their rationale and a link to the definition editor; they are excluded from individual and bulk approval. Rejected findings still require explicit re-research. Assigning an active recipe makes a previously dependency-blocked fertilizing activity eligible again. New research supersedes old unresolved pending findings while retaining history. Approved or superseded history without an actual cadence does not stand in for a configured decision. Progress distinguishes interval/N/A recommendations from unresolved findings, including older completed jobs.

**Research Missing Cadences** queues one priority job per eligible definition in active collections with AI features and AI Curator enabled. Existing active work is retained. Enable AI Curator and run the existing `ai-curator` Docker worker; the browser can close. Priority 1000 puts cadence jobs ahead of ordinary queued research, without interrupting an in-progress call. Existing model, credentials, token settings, budgets, usage tracking and retry settings apply. Worker wakes serialize across processes. Provider requests time out after two minutes; interrupted cadence leases recover after ten minutes with bounded attempts and backoff. Exhausted jobs require a deliberate Retry. Cancel prevents an in-flight response from publishing proposals; provider costs already incurred remain recorded.

Magic Fill husbandry includes the same structured recommendation instructions and validation in its existing request. On saved definitions it persists missing recommendations separately and previews them. Server administrators can select and approve specific recommendations. Saving husbandry alone does not approve cadences. Unsaved definitions need to be saved first. Existing **Research now** also queues missing cadence research through AI Curator. Fertilizer recipe assignment and descriptive husbandry research retain their responsibilities; they no longer write AI-generated numeric fertilizer cadence fields as a side effect.

Research uses botanical identity/taxonomy, description, structured husbandry and the assigned recipe, excluding specimen/acquisition/user details. Recommendations include confidence, rationale, references and applicability. Fixed watering intervals are cautious starting points: inspect moisture and account for substrate, light, pot, temperature, humidity and season. A condition-based activity may be not applicable or have insufficient information. References are model-supplied, not independently verified; prompts prohibit invented citations. No live AI call is necessary for installation.

**Approve** allows editing the approved interval and unit while retaining the original recommendation. **Reject** preserves the result without scheduling it. **Approve All Eligible** first fetches the current eligible set and shows its actual proposal and distinct-definition counts in a cancellable confirmation. It applies only that confirmed set, rechecking each record transactionally; results distinguish approvals and skip reasons. A recipe removed after preview causes a skip. Manual cadence configuration supersedes conflicting pending proposals. Database locks, unique constraints and missing-only inserts protect against simultaneous manual changes and approvals. Bulk operations can partially succeed and can safely be retried.

An active same-collection fertilizer recipe must already be assigned directly to the definition for a scheduled fertilizing recommendation/approval. Available collection recipes and a proposed draft assignment do not qualify. Research without a recipe may recommend justified N/A or leave the activity unresolved. Previous rejections, not-applicable and insufficient results suppress routine spending. Use explicit **Re-research** for a selected definition to revisit those decisions; first review any pending actionable proposals. Eligible N/A recommendations may be approved as explicit nonrecurring decisions or rejected. Insufficient findings cannot activate care; no zero-day interval is used.

## Legacy investigation and recovery

Repository history before `e2d898d` calculated watering from broad text categories (3/5/7/10/18 days, default 7), pest inspections from susceptibility/pest names (14/21/default 30 days), and repotting from prose with approximate day conversions. Those watering/pest guesses were computed at queue-read time, not stored as definition cadence records. Fertilizing used numeric guide/recipe fields or parsed frequency text. The inherited cadence migration retained the husbandry fields, migrated explicit numeric settings, and stopped the implicit guesses.

The existing local `backups/axildb.dump` was inspected read-only with a compatible PostgreSQL client. Its archive dates to August 24, 2026 and contains 23 husbandry guides, including explicit watering, repotting and fertilizer frequency text. It predates AI Curator tables. No production database was inspected or modified and the archive was not restored into any application database. Existing AI Curator stores suggested JSON and Magic Fill stores approved husbandry text; older raw Magic Fill responses were not archived by the endpoint. Backup manifests and migration artifacts contain no separate schedule store.

**Recover previous cadence data** scans retained definition husbandry fields. It extracts unambiguous positive integer day/week/month/year intervals from `wateringCadence`, `repottingInterval`, `fertilizationFrequency`, or numeric `fertilizationCadenceDays`. Ambiguous ranges, descriptive categories and invalid values are skipped. Fertilizing additionally requires the assigned active recipe and an unpaused legacy guide. Existing configured cadences and pending proposals are preserved. Repeated recovery uses a stable original-value fingerprint to avoid duplicates, even after rejection. Results are labeled **Recovered from previous cadence data**, with the original field/text in the rationale; review is mandatory. No new model-generated value is described as recovered.

Recovery also inspects retained AI Curator husbandry/fertilizer suggestion JSON for explicit intervals, retaining the original suggestion ID in its rationale. It never treats an unapplied draft as an approved cadence.

Recovery does not import an archive or restore missing definitions. If current source fields have been cleared, restore a backup into a separate, access-controlled database, inspect only the relevant original definition/guide records, and prepare a separately reviewed import mapping original definition IDs before using recovery. Do not restore a backup over production to recover care text. The archive may contain older values and sensitive unrelated information. Non-numeric prose and runtime guesses cannot be faithfully recovered as stored intervals.

The migration adds proposal constraints, one pending proposal per activity, one active cadence job per definition, a manual-configuration supersession trigger, and changes the older Curator scope index to cover active jobs only so explicit re-research can retain terminal history.

`npm run check:cadence-research` uses a disposable migrated database to exercise the real services with a deterministic provider stub, including restart recovery, budgeted worker integration, usage tracking, concurrent/edited approvals, partial batches, recovery and inheritance. `npm run check:cadence-research:ui` exercises administrator review and permissions against the local test app. Real provider response quality requires review with your configured model; automated tests do not spend tokens or validate external botanical sources.

## Dashboard review integration

For server administrators, the existing **Open review items** dashboard panel includes **Care cadence suggestions** alongside AI Curator and the existing review categories. It counts individual actionable recommendations in the current collection, adds them to the overall item count and counts only nonempty known queues. Cards sort by descending count with stable ties. Zero-count cards are omitted; the existing hidden-panel empty behavior is preserved. Two cards use two columns at tablet/desktop widths, stack on mobile, and keep the same light/dark badge and focus treatment. The Open reviews statistic links to this panel.

The cadence card opens `/server/care-cadence-research?tab=review&collection=<slug>`. Its collection filter persists across tabs and filters, and **Approve All Eligible** previews only that collection. The server still requires verified administrator access; supplying a collection parameter grants no permission. Existing global research/recovery buttons retain their global scope.

A shared eligibility definition excludes resolved statuses, unresolved outcomes, already-configured activities, invalid interval/N/A combinations and recommendations linked to unfinished/failed/cancelled research. Standalone Magic Fill/recovered proposals and completed research results are eligible. Scheduled fertilizing additionally requires an active assigned recipe belonging to the definition's collection; N/A does not. The dashboard uses Prisma `count` with indexed pending/activity predicates and relation existence checks, without selecting recommendation text, sources or full plant records. The review list and bulk eligibility use the same rules; blocked/stale pending proposals remain accessible in History without rewriting their status just to load a page.

Individual cadence approval/rejection, bulk review and manual cadence edits already invalidate the root layout; AI Curator review now also invalidates it. Counts reconcile from the server on navigation/reload, with no new polling interval or optimistic decrements. Work completed in another browser or by background research appears on the next refresh/navigation.

Research count sources fail independently. The panel retains available queues, marks unavailable counts and labels totals as known/partial rather than claiming a complete total or an empty backlog. Reload retries both sources. No migration, research job creation or automatic approval occurs as part of this integration.

Validation: `npm run check:dashboard-reviews` covers rendering/aggregation, singular/plural and empty behavior, sorting, authorization gating, partial failures/retry and eligibility. `npm run check:dashboard-reviews:ui` requires the disposable local database on `localhost:55439/axildb` and test app on port 3107; it verifies actual count queries and mutations, collection scope, back-navigation invalidation, controlled missing-table failures and recovery, keyboard navigation and desktop/tablet/mobile light/dark layouts. It restores test tables and removes seeded data afterward. Never run this failure-injection suite against production.

## Explicit not applicable decisions

Definition care records now store `status=SCHEDULED` or `NOT_APPLICABLE`. Missing records still mean Not scheduled; the migration never converts missing data or existing disabled instance overrides into N/A. Instance overrides retain the existing `OVERRIDE` (scheduled) and `DISABLED` (unresolved) modes and add `NOT_APPLICABLE`; resetting deletes the override. N/A is valid only for Fertilizing, Repotting and Pruning, enforced by shared validation and database constraints. N/A records have null interval/unit. Existing scheduled records retain their intervals with the default SCHEDULED status.

The compact editor supports the three decisions, including N/A without a fertilizer recipe. Only the Scheduled fertilizer option requires an active assigned recipe. Definition and specimen panels and research completeness statistics use `evaluateCareScheduleCompleteness`: all six decisions must be resolved, and Watering, Pest inspection and General inspection must be scheduled. Completeness never blocks ordinary record creation. Legacy disabled overrides remain unresolved.

`resolveCadence` supplies one authoritative effective state for inheritance, derived Care Queue tasks, instance due dates and care digests. N/A returns no recurring cadence or due date, so outstanding derived tasks disappear on the next queue load without deleting completed care events or independent manual tasks. An instance override can schedule care over a definition N/A or suppress an inherited schedule with N/A. Manual care remains available and does not change the decision. Returning to Scheduled uses the existing last-completion/initial-date rules, with one derived item per specimen/activity. Saving a nonrecurring instance decision clears its stale initial due and snooze. Manual decisions supersede pending recommendations under the existing cadence lock; audit metadata records previous values and the new decision.

Research and Magic Fill share structured applicability values: RECOMMENDED (scheduled interval), NOT_APPLICABLE (eligible activity, null interval/unit, botanical rationale), and unresolved outcomes. A missing recipe does not itself imply N/A; it blocks only a scheduled fertilizing recommendation. Existing configured scheduled/N/A decisions are excluded even from explicit re-research; changing a decision requires the normal editor. Standalone Magic Fill and completed research proposals enter the same approval queue. Dashboard counts and bulk previews include actionable N/A alongside intervals, exclude unresolved findings, and revalidate current state, research completion and recipe dependencies on approval. Approval retains reasoning, source, reviewer and timestamp; N/A approval retains null approved interval/unit. Older findings and approved history remain intact, including historical nonactionable N/A findings for required activities; these cannot be approved.

### Migration and rollout

Migration `20261019010000_not_applicable_cadences` adds the explicit status and adjusts nullability/constraints, recipe validation and legacy sync. Apply through `prisma migrate deploy`; do not use db push/reset or modify previously applied migrations. Build and deploy app, migration service and existing AI worker together using the established deployment procedure. No automatic research, approval, database restore or data cleanup occurs during migration.

**Older deployment agents require manual host review for this release.** Updated agents support explicit SQL review and approval through Application Updates; see docs/APPLICATION_UPDATES.md for the one-time agent update. Its conservative migration scanner detects constraint replacement (`DROP CONSTRAINT`) and the updated legacy sync function. Do not bypass the scanner. Use the updated web review flow, or review and back up the host deployment before the documented manual deployment process. Agent code/configuration does not need changing. No production migration or deployment was performed during implementation. Returning to older application code after N/A records exist is not supported without a separately reviewed compatibility plan; do not automatically reverse this migration or restore an older database.
